💡 WHY THIS HAPPENS:

LVGL 9.40 changed lv_obj_get_style_opa() API:
  • Old: Accepted int (e.g., 0)
  • New: Requires lv_part_t enum (e.g., LV_PART_MAIN)

EEZ Studio templates haven't been updated yet for LVGL 9.40.
This toolset bridges the gap.
